KOMAL ART GALLERY — FREE FAQ CHATBOT

This chatbot is completely client-side:
- No API key
- No monthly fee
- No account required
- No visitor questions are sent to an outside AI service

Modified website files:
1. index.html — chatbot interface
2. styles.css — chatbot design and mobile layout
3. script.js — automatic answers and chatbot behaviour

To change an answer later:
Open script.js and find "const chatbotAnswers".
Edit the answer text between the quotation marks, save, commit and redeploy.

The assistant currently answers common questions about:
- Worldwide shipping
- Custom portraits and commissioned artwork
- Prices and quotations
- Online and in-person art classes
- Certificates
- Antiques
- Artwork types
- Gurpreet Singh Komal and records
- Location and contact details
- Buying, payments and completion times
- Careers and visiting hours

Unrecognized questions direct the visitor to the website contact form or WhatsApp.
